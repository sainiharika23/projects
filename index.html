📖 History of Chess

Chess originated in India around the 6th century as Chaturanga. Over the centuries, it evolved through Persia, the Arab world, and Europe, becoming the game we know today.

♜ Chess Pieces ♞

Each player starts with 16 pieces:
1 King, 1 Queen, 2 Rooks, 2 Bishops, 2 Knights, and 8 Pawns.
